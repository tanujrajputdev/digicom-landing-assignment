Simple by Design. Perfect Every Time.

Two products, one principle. Choose your style below and see exactly how it works.

  1. 01

    Freeze the Chill Cradle™

    Detach the Chill Cradle™ from the base and place it in your freezer for at least 3 hours until frozen solid. Keep a spare ready for back-to-back glasses.

  2. 02

    Attach to the Base

    Snap the frozen cradle onto the weighted base. The magnetic connection seats it firmly, so the whole thing lifts and sets down as one piece.

  3. 03

    Rest Your Glass & Pour

    Set your wine glass inside the Chill Cradle™. Pour and enjoy — lift the glass to sip as normal. Nothing enters the glass. Pure, undiluted flavor.

  4. 04

    Wipe & Refreeze

    Detach the cradle, wipe it with a dry towel, and return it to the freezer. Ready for your next glass — tonight, tomorrow, indefinitely.
